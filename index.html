Terms of Use & Privacy

1. What GradePath does

GradePath is an independent study-planning tool. It is not an official school gradebook and is not operated by your school.

2. Accuracy and responsibility

Calculations depend on the grades, assessment weights and grading rules you enter. The tool may not account for category weighting, dropped assignments, curves, extra credit or other school-specific rules. Check important results with your teacher or school. You are responsible for the information you enter and for checking the results before relying on them.

3. Privacy and information you enter

GradePath does not sell or publish the profile or grade information you enter to the general public. In this version, your name, grade, school and course information are stored locally in this browser and are not uploaded to a GradePath account or database. The static website host may process ordinary technical request information when the website loads; this is separate from the profile and grades you enter.

4. Shared devices and backups

Anyone with access to the same browser profile or device may be able to see locally saved information. Avoid entering information you do not want another user of that device to see. Clearing browser data can erase your profile and courses. Use the Export button to download a backup and keep it somewhere private.

5. No account or cross-device sync

This version does not provide sign-in, cloud storage or automatic syncing. Your information stays in the browser where you entered it unless you export and import a backup yourself.
